Atraly for Robotics

You build the body. We provide the mind.

Atraly is the AI mind inside the machine — personal intelligence that knows the people it serves, talks naturally, and gets things done. Atraly for Robotics puts that mind inside your hardware, so you can ship years faster instead of spending years building AI yourself.

What your robot gets

Personal intelligence. Atraly knows the household it serves — routines, preferences, people. That knowledge carries into your robot from day one.

Conversation. Natural talk, not commands. Atraly speaks like a person, so your robot feels like one.

Workers that do things. Atraly’s workers plan jobs and work through them — “clean the kitchen” becomes a plan, not a script.

Powered by Atraly. Partner robots carry the badge, and your customers get the AI they already know.

How it works

We work directly with manufacturers to integrate Atraly into your hardware and software stack. Your team builds the body — we bring the mind.
