AI security audits

Aorila reviews AI systems the way we review our own stack: what the model can do, where data moves, and what happens when it fails.

This page is informational. Engagements are scoped in writing before any work begins. We do not publish pricing. Model A remains the only product we sell.

What an audit covers

Typical work includes one or more of the following, chosen after a short scoping call:

Model and tool-use review — prompts, tools, retrieval, and agent loops that can leak data or take unintended actions.

Data handling — training, logs, embeddings, third-party processors, retention, and deletion paths.

Access and tenancy — auth, session handling, isolation between customers, and admin surfaces.

Deployment posture — secrets, supply chain, inference endpoints, and what is exposed on the public internet.

Incident readiness — what you can reconstruct after a failure, and who is notified.

How we work

We start with a written scope: systems in play, data classes, and the questions you need answered. Findings come back as a private report with severity, evidence, and a recommended order of work. Pricing is quoted after that scope is agreed.

Aorila does not publish client names from audits unless you ask us to.
