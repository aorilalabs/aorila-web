Company

Aorila is a robotics company. Our mission is to bring advanced robotics into every home — a user-friendly robot with an intuitive interface that helps people with everyday tasks.

What we’re building

Our first product is Model A, a home robot: friendly, capable, and zero-setup — designed to help with everyday tasks around the house. The presale is open now at robotics.aorila.com: a $50 refundable deposit reserves your place, or $4,999 buys it outright. No subscription, ever.

Who we are

Aorila was founded by Nicholas Baldwin and Jordan Baldwin and is headquartered in Columbia, South Carolina.

For anything about the company or the robot, write to info@aorila.com.
