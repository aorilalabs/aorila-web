05 / FAQ

Honest answers.

What can I use today?

Atraly — our personal AI — is ready today and free to start. Your console account is ready too; the GPU catalog opens as hosts onboard, so create an account now and you'll be first in.

How does billing work?

1 credit = $1. Usage meters by the minute against your credit balance, and workloads stop automatically at zero — so you can never overspend.

Where do the GPUs come from?

Individuals and businesses who list their own GPU hardware on Aorila's peer-powered marketplace — 100% compliant supply, no third-party cloud resale. Aorila takes a cut; you get one $/hour price, per-minute metering, and one Aorila bill.

What about the home robot?

Our home robot is in development — zero setup, made to help with everyday tasks. Meet the home robot.

Who is behind Aorila?

Aorila was founded by Nicholas Baldwin and Jordan Baldwin and is headquartered in Columbia, SC. Reach us at info@aorila.com.

What about my privacy?

Trust and security is our first requirement. Your workloads and your data stay yours.
