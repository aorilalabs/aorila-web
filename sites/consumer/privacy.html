Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Aorila (“Aorila,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use aorila.com.

Effective September 11, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the public Aorila website at aorila.com and related pages we host on this domain for company information and API access requests. It does not replace privacy notices for Atraly, Ally, Aorila Labs, or other products that publish their own policies. This site is not a consumer account product and does not process payments.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details and message content you send to api@aorila.com or through an access request path on this site

We do not require you to create an account to read these pages.

3. How we use information

We use this information to respond to inquiries, evaluate API access requests, operate and secure the site, improve reliability, detect abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information collected on this site, and we do not use it to build third-party advertising profiles.

4. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us host the site, process email, store request records, or provide security, under obligations to use the information only for those purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect Aorila or others, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

5. Retention

We retain correspondence and request records as needed to respond, support ongoing relationships, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Technical logs are typically kept for a shorter operational period unless needed for security or compliance.

6. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards appropriate for a standard company website and inbound request flow. No online transmission or storage method is perfectly secure.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use limited cookies or similar technologies required for basic functionality. We do not run third-party advertising cookies on aorila.com.

8. Your choices

You can contact us at api@aorila.com to ask about updating or deleting information you sent us. We may retain limited records when required by law or for legitimate purposes such as security and fraud prevention. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

9. Children

This site is directed to a general adult and professional audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through aorila.com.

10. International visitors

If you visit from another country, your information may be processed in locations with different data-protection rules than those where you live.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: api@aorila.com.

Commercial Labs requests are covered separately at aorilalabs.com/privacy.
