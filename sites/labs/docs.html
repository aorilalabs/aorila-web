RENTERS

How billing works

GPU time is metered per minute from your credit balance. 1 credit = $1. Workloads stop automatically at $0 — you can’t overspend.

HOSTS

How payouts work

List hardware you own, set your per-minute price, and accrue payouts per minute of rented time. Aorila takes a small marketplace fee. Host onboarding is open now.

POLICY

What’s not allowed

Cryptominers, malware, and spam are excluded by policy. If a workload isn’t legitimate compute, it doesn’t run here.
