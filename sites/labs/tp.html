TERMS OF SERVICE

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 16, 2026.

1. Services

Aorila Labs provides on-demand GPU compute (“Pods”) through aorilalabs.com and the Aorila Labs dashboard. Compute is billed per minute against prepaid credits: 1 credit equals $1 of compute. The per-minute rate for each offer is shown before you launch. When your balance reaches zero, running workloads stop automatically — you can never owe more than you have added.

2. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials and API tokens confidential, and for all activity under your account. You must provide accurate contact information and keep it up to date.

3. Acceptable use

You agree not to: (a) use the services for any unlawful purpose; (b) mine cryptocurrency on rented compute; (c) resell, white-label, or redistribute the services without our prior written agreement; (d) interfere with other customers’ workloads or with the operation of the platform; or (e) attempt to gain unauthorized access to our systems or data.

4. Credits

Credits are prepaid and non-refundable. They are tied to your account, are non-transferable, and can never be withdrawn, exchanged for cash, or redeemed for cash value. Credits remain on your account until used.

5. Payments and disputes

Credit purchases are processed by our payment provider. By purchasing credits you authorize us to charge your selected payment method for the full amount. If you dispute a charge through your bank or card issuer (a chargeback), we will suspend your account pending review and remove the disputed credits.

6. Availability

The services are provided on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability: compute supply comes from a marketplace of independent hosts and capacity may vary. No service-level agreement applies unless separately agreed in writing.

7. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Aorila Labs is not liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising from your use of the services. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for credits in the twelve months preceding the claim.

8. Termination and suspension

We may suspend or terminate your account at any time for violation of these terms, suspected fraud, or a chargeback. You may close your account at any time; unused credits are non-refundable.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of the services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

10. Governing law

11. Contact

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026.

What we collect

Account information: your email address when you sign in or create an account. Host applications: name, email, hardware models and counts, and availability, which we use to review applications and follow up. Billing records: credit purchases and per-minute usage metering, so we can charge correctly and show you what ran. Support messages: whatever you send us when you ask for help.

Payments

Credit purchases are processed by our payment providers (Stripe and Lemon Squeezy). They handle your card or payment details directly — we receive transaction records (amount, date, last four digits where provided) but we never receive or store full card numbers.

Analytics and cookies

Our marketing pages use Google Analytics 4 (property G-68Z6SM8C4B), which collects page views, approximate location, device and browser information. We use it to understand which pages are useful and where the site breaks. We also use cookies for sign-in sessions and basic preferences, and analytics cookies as described above. Blocking cookies may break sign-in.

How we use it

To run the marketplace: accounts, metering, billing, payouts to hosts, fraud prevention, and support. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with the providers who process payments, host our infrastructure, and provide analytics — each limited to what they need to do their job.

Data retention

We keep account and billing records while your account is active and as long as tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention rules require afterward. Host applications are kept while under review and for a reasonable period after. Analytics data is retained under our analytics provider’s settings. You can request deletion of your account data at any time.

Your rights and contact

You can ask for a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: info@aorila.com.
