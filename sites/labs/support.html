SUPPORT

Support.

These terms cover Aorila Compute on aorilalabs.com.

BILLING

Questions about a bill

Compute is billed per minute at 1 credit = $1. Workloads stop automatically when your balance hits $0, so a bill can’t grow past what you’ve funded. Host payouts accrue per minute of rented time, minus Aorila’s small marketplace fee.

Billing disputes: api@aorila.com.

HOSTS

Host applications

Applying through the host form is an application, not a listing. Listings are subject to review, and every host must list hardware they own. Questions about hosting: api@aorila.com.

Trust and security is number one.
