Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Aorila (“Aorila,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use aorilalabs.com.

Effective September 11, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the Aorila Labs website and the commercial API access request form. It does not cover Aorila product accounts, third-party apps, or other Aorila properties that publish their own notices. This site is not a consumer account product and does not process payments.

2. Information we collect

When you submit the access request form, we collect:

  • Company name
  • Contact name
  • Work email address
  • Use case description
  • Volume estimate
  • Website, if you choose to provide one

If you email api@aorila.com, we collect the content of that correspondence and related metadata.

We also collect ordinary technical logs when you visit the site, which may include approximate time, requested path, user agent, and network address. These logs help us operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Review and respond to access requests
  • Communicate about eligibility, onboarding, and support
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Labs site
  • Detect abuse, spam, and security issues
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the Labs form to run advertising profiles.

4. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us host infrastructure, store leads, deliver email, or secure the site, under obligations to use the information only for those purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect Aorila or others, or in connection with a corporate transaction such as a merger or asset sale.

5. Retention

We keep request and correspondence records for as long as needed to evaluate access, support ongoing commercial relationships, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Technical logs are retained for a shorter operational period unless needed for security or compliance.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate for a standard AI company website and lead intake flow. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The Labs site may use limited cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for basic site function (for example, remembering a preview site preference on shared hosts). We do not operate third-party advertising cookies on this site.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form. If you have already submitted information and want to update or delete a request, email api@aorila.com. We may retain limited records when required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. To exercise those rights, contact us using the email above.

9. Children

Aorila Labs is directed to businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through this site.

10. International visitors

If you access the site from outside the country where we operate our systems, your information may be processed in other locations that may have different data-protection rules.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: api@aorila.com.

For the consumer Aorila site, see the privacy notice on aorila.com/privacy.
