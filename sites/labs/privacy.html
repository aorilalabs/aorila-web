Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy covers aorilalabs.com. The site is a commercial request-access form for Aorila’s API. It is not an account product.

Effective 10 September 2026

What we collect

If you use the request form, we collect:

  • Company
  • Contact name
  • Work email
  • Use case
  • Volume estimate
  • Website, if you provide one

If you email api@aorila.com, we collect the address and the message. The site also keeps ordinary request logs: time, path, and network address.

We do not create Labs accounts on this host. We do not take payment on this host.

How we use it

We use the form to evaluate access requests and to reply by email. We do not sell that information.

Contact

Privacy questions: api@aorila.com.
