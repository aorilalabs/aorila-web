GPU Pods

On-demand GPU containers from community hosts and verified providers, billed per minute — never by the hour.

Pick a GPU from the marketplace in the dashboard, choose a template or bring your own container, and get SSH and Jupyter endpoints. When you stop the pod, billing stops. Your pod runs on a compute marketplace: individuals and businesses list their own hardware and set their own prices, and you pay one simple bill.

Launch a pod

CPU Instances

CPU-only virtual machines for the things that don't need a GPU: APIs, websites, databases, background workers.

Available in 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64 vCPU tiers. Anyone can list a machine — even a basic office PC — so prices start low and you pick the host that fits. If your workload outgrows CPU, the same dashboard rents you a GPU pod.

Launch an instance

Serverless Endpoints

Autoscaling GPU inference that scales to zero. You pay only for the milliseconds you actually run.

Deploy a model as an HTTPS endpoint. Traffic spikes? It scales up. Nobody calling it at 3am? It scales to zero and you pay nothing. Built for production AI features where a pod sitting idle would be money on fire.

Deploy an endpoint

Clusters

Multi-GPU bare metal for training runs. Reserve capacity by the hour or the day.

When one GPU isn't enough, reserve a whole cluster. You get the raw machines, on fast interconnect, for as long as your run needs — hours or days. Bring your own distributed training setup.

Storage Volumes

Persistent network and NVMe storage that attaches to any pod. Your data survives restarts.

Pods are ephemeral by design — stop one and its local disk goes with it. Attach a volume and your datasets, checkpoints, and project files persist across pods. Detach and reattach to a bigger GPU whenever you want.

Create a volume

Hub

One-click templates and hosted open-weight models: notebooks, LLMs, image models, inference servers. Deploy in seconds — no setup, no servers.

Hub is the fast lane. Instead of configuring a pod by hand, pick what you want running — a Jupyter notebook, an open-weight LLM with an API, an image model — and it's live in seconds. Everything in Hub is also reproducible: the same template runs on any pod, so what you prototype is what you ship.

BILLING

How billing works

One credit is one dollar. Everything is billed per minute. When your balance hits zero, workloads stop — you can never go negative.

Top up credits in the dashboard. Every product above draws from the same balance, and every charge is itemized per minute so you always know what ran and what it cost.
