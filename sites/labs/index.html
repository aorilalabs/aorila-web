FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does billing work?

By the minute, not by the hour. 1 credit = $1. Your workload stops automatically at $0, so you can never spend more than you loaded.

What can I run on Aorila Labs?

GPU Pods are live today — rent a GPU by the minute for AI training and inference. CPU instances, serverless endpoints, multi-GPU clusters, and persistent storage volumes are on the way.

Do I need a GPU to earn as a host?

No. Any machine that clears the bar — 4+ threads, 8 GB RAM, 10 GB free disk, stable broadband — can list, including a basic office PC. Hosts with GPUs earn more; hosts without one still earn. You set the price and your availability.

How do hosts get paid?

Earnings meter by the minute while your machine serves workloads. Track everything in the dashboard.

Do I need an account to get started?

Yes — create a free account to open the dashboard. From there you can launch compute, host a machine, and manage credits and API keys.
