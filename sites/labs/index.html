01 / HOW IT WORKS

Two sides. One marketplace.

Hosts list hardware; renters get GPUs. Aorila runs the marketplace in between — one catalog, one job API, one bill.

FOR HOSTS

List your GPUs. Get paid per minute.

Own a GPU? List the hardware you already have — you set your per-minute price and payouts accrue per minute of rented time. Aorila takes a small marketplace fee; we carry zero upfront capacity cost, and so do you. Every listing is hardware the host owns: no third-party cloud resale, ever.

FOR RENTERS

Rent GPUs. One simple bill.

Browse the catalog, deploy a pod, and pay per minute from your Aorila credit balance. 1 credit = $1. Your workload stops automatically when your balance hits $0 — you can’t wake up to a surprise bill.

02 / BILLING

Billing that can’t surprise you.

One honest unit of money. Metered by the minute. With a hard stop at zero.

Per-minute billing

HARD STOP

03 / TRUST & SECURITY

Trust and security is number one.

You hand us your workloads and your spend. That trust is our first engineering requirement — not a feature.

SUPPLY

Compliant supply, only

Every host lists hardware they own — individuals and businesses, peer to peer. No third-party cloud resale. One hundred percent of marketplace supply is peer-listed hardware.

POLICY

Clean marketplace

Cryptominers, malware, and spam are excluded by policy. If a workload isn’t legitimate compute, it doesn’t run here.

PLATFORM

Fail-closed by default

Production won’t start without operator credentials — there is no open mode. Raw provider errors never reach customers.

BILLING

One bill, no leaks

You never touch a host’s account and never see a host’s name. One Aorila bill covers everything you run.
