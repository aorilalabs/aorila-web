01 / HOW IT WORKS

Two sides. One marketplace.

Hosts list hardware; renters get GPUs. Aorila runs the marketplace in between — one catalog, one job API, one bill.

FOR HOSTS

List your GPUs. Get paid per minute.

Own a GPU? List the hardware you already have — you set your per-minute price and payouts accrue per minute of rented time. Aorila takes a small marketplace fee; we carry zero upfront capacity cost, and so do you.

FOR RENTERS

Rent GPUs. One simple bill.

Pick a live GPU offer, deploy a pod, and pay per minute from your Aorila credit balance. 1 credit = $1. Your workload stops automatically when your balance hits $0 — you can’t wake up to a surprise bill.
