VERIFICATION SPEC · V1

Trust, verified. Here’s exactly how.

Every GPU on Aorila Compute passes the same verification before it can be listed — and carries a public reliability score after. This page is the whole program: what we check, how tiers are earned, how billing protects you, and what happens when something goes wrong. Nothing hidden, nothing promised we don’t do.

01 / THE GATE

No score, no listing.

A host GPU can’t appear in the catalog until it proves what it is and how fast it is. Verification is the price of admission — not an optional badge.

MANDATORY

Standardized benchmark, before listing

Every host GPU runs the same standardized benchmark during onboarding. The score is recorded against the listing and re-checked over time. If a GPU can’t complete the benchmark, it doesn’t list — no exceptions, no manual overrides.

ATTESTATION

Prove the hardware you claim

Hosts attest GPU model, VRAM, and driver version at onboarding, and the agent confirms it against what the machine actually reports. A listing that says RTX 4090 is an RTX 4090 — not a relabeled card, not a shared slice.

02 / WHAT WE CHECK

Five checks. Always on.

Verification doesn’t end at onboarding. Every listed GPU is watched for the whole time it’s on the marketplace.

ONBOARDING

Benchmark

One standardized benchmark every GPU must pass before its first listing. The result sets the baseline everything else is measured against.

CONTINUOUS

Heartbeat

The host agent reports in continuously. A GPU that goes silent stops being bookable — listings never sell time on a machine that isn’t there.

PERIODIC

Re-benchmarking

Benchmarks re-run on a schedule. A GPU that quietly degrades — throttling, failing memory, swapped hardware — loses its score and its placement.

INTEGRITY

Anti-spoofing

We check for virtualized, duplicated, or benchmark-gamed GPUs. Hardware that isn’t what it claims to be is removed from the catalog.

IDENTITY

Attestation

GPU model, VRAM, and driver are attested at onboarding and re-confirmed. What the listing says is what the machine is.

OVERSIGHT

Removal

Failing, fraudulent, or policy-violating hosts are demoted or removed. Trust is a listing requirement, not a reputation game.

03 / RELIABILITY SCORE

Every listing carries its record.

Each host GPU gets a public reliability score built from uptime and benchmark history — shown right on the listing, sortable, filterable. Good hosts earn better placement. Bad ones don’t hide.

PUBLIC

On the listing, not behind it

Uptime and benchmark history are visible to every renter before they deploy. You choose hardware with its record attached — never blind.

EARNED

Built over time, lost fast

Scores rise with sustained uptime and consistent benchmark results — and drop the moment a host degrades, disappears, or games the system.

ACTIONABLE

Sort by what matters

Filter and sort the catalog by reliability, not just price. The cheapest GPU is only cheap if it finishes your job.

04 / HOST TIERS

Earn your badge.

Three tiers, each one harder to get and worth more placement. Tiers are earned by evidence — never bought, never granted by hand.

TIER 1

New

Every host starts here: benchmarked, attested, and listed — but unproven. New-tier listings are bookable with their score visible from day one.

TIER 2

Verified

Earned through sustained uptime and a clean benchmark history over time. Verified hosts get priority placement and the Verified badge on every listing.

TIER 3

Certified

For businesses and fleets: Verified history plus business-entity confirmation and facility evidence — power, cooling, and network you can point to. Certified capacity gets top placement for serious workloads.

05 / BILLING PROTECTION

You pay for minutes that ran.

Metering is escrow-style: time is measured as it’s used, and money only moves for minutes that actually ran.

PER-MINUTE

Metered by the minute

GPU time is measured per minute, not rounded up to the hour. Short job, short bill — on both sides of the marketplace.

1 CREDIT = $1

One honest unit

Renters spend credits at 1 credit = $1. No hidden breakdowns, no provider names on your bill — one Aorila bill covers everything.

HARD STOP

Workloads stop at $0

When a renter’s balance runs out, workloads stop automatically. You can’t spend what you don’t have — and hosts are never left chasing payment.

06 / DISPUTES & REFUNDS

When something goes wrong, there’s a path.

Hardware fails and hosts disappear. The program doesn’t pretend otherwise — it gives renters a way to report it and get made whole.

RENTERS

Report, investigate, refund

If a host underperforms, vanishes mid-job, or wasn’t the hardware it claimed, renters can open a dispute. We investigate against the host’s benchmark and heartbeat record — and verified failures are refunded from the renter’s side.

HOSTS

Failures have consequences

Hosts behind verified disputes lose reliability score, drop tiers, and can be demoted or removed from the catalog. Repeat offenders don’t get a fourth chance.

What we don’t claim

We don’t claim third-party audits, ISO certifications, or insurance coverage we don’t have. This spec is v1 — it describes the program as it runs today, and it grows in public as we add independent verification.

Trust and security is number one.
