Aorila Labs is the compute arm of Aorila.

WHAT WE DO

A compute marketplace, not a cloud.

We don’t own a datacenter. Aorila Labs is a marketplace where individuals and businesses list their own GPUs, CPUs, and storage, set their own prices, and get paid by the minute — and where anyone can rent that compute on one simple bill.

One credit is one dollar. Everything is billed per minute, and workloads stop at $0, so you can never spend more than you loaded. Hosts keep 100% of the price they set; a 5% platform fee is added on top for renters, never taken out of host earnings.

Trust and security is number one. That’s the whole model: real supply, honest prices, one bill.
